Common questions

Who actually does the work?

Akash, the founder, writes the code on every project. If a project ever needs extra hands, you'll know who they are and approve them before they start.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work in English, over email, video calls and shared project boards, and keep a few hours of overlap with your working day.

Who owns the code?

You do. Code goes into your repository, and hosting and third-party accounts are set up in your name from the start.

Can you take over an existing project?

Usually, yes. We start with a short paid review of the codebase, so we can both see what we're dealing with before committing to anything bigger.

Not sure which of these you need?

Describe the problem instead. We'll suggest the approach.
