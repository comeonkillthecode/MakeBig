About MakeBig

The story behind MakeBig Digital Solutions.

MakeBig Digital Solutions was founded with a simple mission — to help businesses of all sizes establish a powerful digital presence. We believe that every company, whether a startup or an enterprise, deserves world-class web solutions that drive real growth.

With over 5 years of hands-on experience in web development, we bring deep technical expertise across the full stack. From building blazing-fast frontends with React and Next.js to architecting scalable backends with Node.js and Java Spring, from launching Shopify stores to creating custom API integrations — we've done it all.

What sets us apart is our founder-led approach. Every project gets the direct attention of a senior developer who understands both the technical details and the business goals. No account managers, no miscommunication — just expert execution delivered on time.
