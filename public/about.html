About

A small studio, on purpose

MakeBig Digital Solutions is a founder-led development studio based in India, working with businesses wherever they are.

Why MakeBig exists

MakeBig started from a simple observation: many businesses pay agency prices and still never speak to the person building their product. Requirements pass through two or three people, and things get lost on the way.

We work the other way round. The person you talk to is the person writing the code. That keeps projects smaller, faster and easier to change halfway through.

After more than five years building frontends, backends, Shopify stores and integrations, the work has moved naturally into AI. That means chatbots that answer from a company's own content, and MCP servers that let assistants like Claude and ChatGPT work with real business systems. It's the same engineering discipline, applied to a new kind of tool.

How we work

What you can expect

  • A direct line

    No account managers. Questions go to the developer and get answered by the developer.

  • Scope in writing

    Before any code: what's included, what isn't, the timeline and the price.

  • Your code, your accounts

    Repositories, hosting and API keys belong to you from day one. Leaving us is easy.

  • Plain answers about AI

    If AI isn't the right tool for your problem, we'll say so before you spend anything on it.

  • Visible progress

    A staging link you can click through and a short written update every week.

  • Built to be handed over

    Readable code, notes on how it works, and tests where they matter, so the next developer isn't starting from zero.
